## What it does

YouTube lets editors add a video while preserving a privacy-first first load. Capell stores the normalized video identifier and renders a local poster until the visitor chooses to play.

## Do I need to do anything?

Add the widget to a page in Layout Builder, complete its fields, and preview the public page before publishing.

## Where it shows up

In Layout Builder, as a YouTube widget for the video identifier, local poster, title, and supporting copy. The privacy-enhanced iframe is created only after activation.

## Good to know

- This free beta extension belongs to Capell Foundation and requires Layout Builder.
- No request reaches YouTube before explicit activation, and Privacy Center can keep the embed disabled when consent is denied.
